## Closing soon: see these first

### [Schiaparelli: Fashion Becomes Art](https://www.vam.ac.uk/exhibitions/schiaparelli)
*V&A South Kensington · closes Sunday, Nov 15, 2026*\
The history and lasting influence of Elsa Schiaparelli, one of the 20th century’s most inventive designers and a close collaborator of the Surrealists. Last chance: under two weeks left.

### [Frida: The Making of an Icon](https://www.tate.org.uk/whats-on/tate-modern/frida-kahlo-the-making-of-an-icon)
*Tate Modern · until Jan 3, 2027*\
How Frida Kahlo became one of the most recognizable artists in the world, through her self-portraits and the image built around her. Expect it to be busy right up to the end.

### [Salman Toor: Someone Like You](https://courtauld.ac.uk/whats-on/exh-salman-toor-someone-like-you/)
*The Courtauld Gallery · until Jan 10, 2027*\
The first solo exhibition in Europe of the New York-based painter Salman Toor. It’s in the same building as the Courtauld’s Impressionist collection, so you can see both in one visit.

### [Ana Mendieta](https://www.tate.org.uk/whats-on/tate-modern/ana-mendieta)
*Tate Modern · until Jan 17, 2027*\
The Cuban-American artist’s work in performance, film and photography, much of it made with her own body in the landscape.

## The big shows: on into 2027

### [Renoir and Love](https://www.nationalgallery.org.uk/exhibitions/renoir-and-love)
*The National Gallery · until Jan 31, 2027*\
Renoir’s gift for painting love, friendship and everyday pleasure. Look out for the gallery’s Friday-evening sessions, which run until mid-January.

### [Painting the French Riviera](https://www.royalacademy.org.uk/exhibition/painting-the-french-riviera)
*Royal Academy of Arts · until Jan 31, 2027*\
How the light and color of the Côte d’Azur drew modern art’s great innovators south.

### [Korea](https://www.britishmuseum.org/exhibitions/korea)
*British Museum · until Jan 31, 2027*\
The British Museum’s major exhibition on Korea. Note that the exhibition is closed on December 1 and 2.

### [The 90s: Art and Fashion](https://www.tate.org.uk/whats-on/tate-britain/the-90s)
*Tate Britain · until Feb 14, 2027*\
Curated by Edward Enninful, whose own experience of the decade shapes it, a look at how art and fashion changed British culture in the 1990s.

### [Light and Magic: The Birth of Art Photography](https://www.tate.org.uk/whats-on/tate-modern/light-and-magic)
*Tate Modern · until Feb 21, 2027*\
Photography’s path to being taken seriously as an art form.

### [Robert Ryman](https://www.barbican.org.uk/whats-on/2026/event/robert-ryman-the-real-thing)
*Barbican Art Gallery · until Feb 28, 2027*\
The American painter who spent his career on white paintings and the textures, edges and supports that make each one different. As Ryman put it, you have to see the real thing; reproductions flatten them completely.

### [The Bayeux Tapestry](https://www.britishmuseum.org/exhibitions/bayeux-tapestry)
*British Museum · until Jul 11, 2027*\
The embroidered story of the Norman Conquest, on loan from France. **Tickets from September 10, 2026 to March 31, 2027 have sold out.** The museum releases further dates in 2027 and has a mailing list for releases, so sign up now if you want to go in the spring or summer.

## Opening this month

### [Constantinople to Istanbul: One City, Two Empires](https://www.vam.ac.uk/exhibitions/constantinople-to-istanbul-one-city-two-empires)
*V&A South Kensington · opens Saturday, Nov 7, 2026*\
1,600 years of art and design from one city, across the Byzantine and Ottoman empires. The V&A bills it as a first for the UK.

### [Vanessa Bell & Duncan Grant: Inside Bloomsbury](https://www.tate.org.uk/whats-on/tate-britain/vanessa-bell-and-duncan-grant)
*Tate Britain · Nov 12, 2026 – Apr 11, 2027*\
The two Bloomsbury painters who lived and worked side by side for decades, and whose interiors and designs were as much a part of their art as their canvases.

### [Van Eyck: The Portraits](https://www.nationalgallery.org.uk/exhibitions/van-eyck-the-portraits)
*The National Gallery · Nov 21, 2026 – Apr 11, 2027*\
For the first time, all of Jan van Eyck’s portraits together, including the gallery’s own *Arnolfini Portrait*. The National Gallery calls it a once-only event. Book early.

## Coming next

Monet fans: the Musée de l’Orangerie’s centenary exhibition *Monet, peindre le temps* moves to **Tate Modern from February 25 to June 27, 2027**. If you can’t get to [Paris](/guides/best-art-exhibitions-paris/) this winter, it’s coming to you.

## Tips for exhibition-going in London

- **Most permanent collections are free; most big exhibitions aren’t.** The National Gallery, Tate, the British Museum and the V&A charge for major shows. Members usually get in free, which pays off quickly if you go to more than two or three shows a year.
- **Use the late openings.** Several of the shows above have Friday-evening hours. They’re the least crowded way to see a blockbuster.
- **Book ahead for the National Gallery and British Museum headliners.** Van Eyck and the Bayeux Tapestry are the two shows this season where walking up on the day is unlikely to work.
- **Check the dates again before you go.** Every link above goes to the museum, so confirm there.
